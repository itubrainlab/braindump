Three new inputs: high-density EEG, a webcam, and OBS over NDI.

## New devices

- **g.tec g.HIamp**: up to 256 EEG channels at 256 to 4800 Hz, through g.Pype's
  GHIamp source and g.tec's gtec-gds driver (Windows, Linux and macOS). The
  channels are named from the project's montage, and the digital input is
  recorded as TRIG, its rising edges becoming the run's trigger events. Its
  timing is measured as the Unicorn's is, so it can be the recording's time
  base. The data viewer shows the channels a page at a time.
- **Webcam**: any camera on the computer.
- **OBS over NDI**: OBS's output (DistroAV plugin), with NDI's low latency, from
  this computer or another on the network. Frames are placed on brAIn dump's
  clock from their sender time stamps. The recording label is `screen` by
  default.

Video follows the BIDS proposal **BEP047** (behavioural audio/video): under
`beh/` as `…_recording-<label>_video.mp4`, with a sidecar giving codec, frame
rate and count, size and camera position, aligned with the other recordings
through `scans.tsv`. Every frame's time is kept as well, in a
`…_recording-<label>_physio.tsv.gz` beside it. BEP047 is not in a BIDS release
yet, so the video files are listed in `.bidsignore`; the dataset validates
without errors.

When two EEG amplifiers record together, each gets its own `acq-` entity
(`acq-unicorn`, `acq-hiamp`).

## Changes

- The toolbar's **Connect all** and **Disconnect all** buttons show a plug
  going into, and out of, its socket.
- Started with `--simulate`, every device page says so, and its **Simulated**
  box is ticked and locked, without changing the project's own setting.

## Install

The Windows zip, the macOS DMG (Apple Silicon, macOS 14+) or the Linux tarball
(x86_64, glibc 2.34+). The first launch downloads g.Pype, g.tec's drivers
(gtec-unicorn, gtec-gds), NDI and the other libraries from PyPI. On macOS the
app asks once for camera access.
