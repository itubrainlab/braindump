A validation procedure for brAIn dump setups, tried on the lab's hardware.
The app itself is unchanged from 0.2.0.

## Validation procedure

[`validation/`](https://github.com/itubrainlab/braindump/tree/main/validation)
checks, in about 10 minutes, that a setup records correctly and in step. It
runs from a source checkout; see its README.

- **`face_validation.py`** (PsychoPy). Every step is sent as an LSL marker,
  stamped at its screen flip:
  - instructions;
  - 30 s resting with the eyes open, then 30 s with them closed;
  - the GP3's calibration, drawn by PsychoPy over the Open Gaze API;
  - 30 faces in random order: a cross at a random place (2 s), the face
    there (1 s), a blank (1 s);
  - a debriefing.
- **`make_project.py`** makes the matching brAIn dump project:
  - the Unicorn;
  - the GP3, read over the Open Gaze API;
  - the procedure's LSL markers;
  - OBS's screen over NDI.
- **`analyse.py`** checks the recording and writes a report with figures:
  - alpha, eyes closed vs open;
  - gaze accuracy at the crosses;
  - the N170 to the faces (average reference);
  - synchronisation: the markers in `eeg/` and `beh/`, saccade latencies,
    gaze loss at eyes closed, the faces' appearance in the screen recording,
    and coverage.

  It also reports each EEG channel's quality, valid gaze by screen height,
  eye loss at the edges, and the shape of OBS's canvas, to explain a
  failure.
- **`screen_delay.py`** measures OBS's NDI delay: the value for the OBS
  input's Delay setting.

## On the lab's hardware

A MacBook with a 27″ screen: a Unicorn, the GP3 under `opengp3 server`, and
OBS over NDI. After the first session's fixes, all of them in the setup:

- OBS's canvas set to the screen's shape;
- its 43 ms delay set in brAIn dump;
- the GP3 tilted up.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Alpha | **Passed** |
| N170 | **Passed**: 172 ms, p < 0.001 |
| Synchronisation | **Passed**: the screen within 5 ms of the markers, saccades 333 ms after each cross |
| Gaze | Median error 1.06°; 66 % of crosses within 1.5°, short of the 80 % asked: the GP3's range at the edges of a 27″ screen |
